# Fictional examples only

Supplier A sells 1 kg spelt flour for €2.40. A recipe consuming 150 g uses €0.36 of flour. Supplier B sells 1 kg skyr for €5.00; 200 g uses €1.00. Packaging per serving is €0.35. Hypothetical sale price is €5.50 gross. These are not real prices and the business is invented. In a non-VAT-registered / gross-cost scenario, contribution per serving before further variable costs is €3.79 and contribution margin ~68.91%. Under a *purely illustrative* 20% output VAT scenario with recoverable input VAT, input-cost values must be stated as net before using the same €1.71 cost sum; the resulting sale revenue is €4.583333..., contribution ~€2.873333..., contribution margin ~62.69%. Do not interpret illustrative tax rates as legal or appropriate for any specific business.
